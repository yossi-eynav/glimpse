# Glimpse Privacy Policy

**Product:** Glimpse
**Publisher:** The developer of Glimpse, an independent developer ("we", "us")
**Contact:** [yossi.eynav@gmail.com](mailto:yossi.eynav@gmail.com)
**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

## Summary

Glimpse shows photos you pick from Google Photos as your Mac's wallpaper and screensaver.
Everything stays on your Mac. We never see your photos, and nothing is sent to us. Glimpse talks
only to Google, and only to sign you in and fetch the photos you picked.

## What Glimpse accesses

When you sign in with Google, Glimpse asks for:

- **The photos you pick.** You choose them in Google's own photo picker. Glimpse gets read-only
  access to those photos only.
- **Your Google account email address.** Glimpse shows it in its menu so you know which account
  is signed in.
- **Sign-in tokens.** Google gives these to Glimpse so it can fetch your picked photos without
  asking you to sign in every time.

Glimpse cannot see the rest of your Google Photos library. It never uploads, changes, or deletes
anything in your library.

## How Glimpse uses it

Glimpse uses this information only to:

- set your picked photos as your wallpaper and, if you turn it on, your screensaver;
- keep working when you are offline, using the copies saved on your Mac;
- show which Google account is signed in.

Glimpse does not use your information for anything else.

## Where it's stored

Everything is stored **only on your Mac**. We do not run any servers, and nothing is stored
with us. On your Mac, Glimpse keeps:

- copies of your picked photos, sized for your screen, and small thumbnails;
- the ID, size, and date of each picked photo, so it knows which photos you chose;
- your settings, such as how often the wallpaper changes;
- the wallpaper you had before Glimpse changed it, so it can put it back when you turn Glimpse
  off;
- a copy of the current photo for the screensaver, if you use the screensaver;
- your sign-in tokens, encrypted with a key kept in your macOS Keychain.

Glimpse does not write log files. Messages it prints while running hide your tokens.

## Who it's shared with

Glimpse sends information **only to Google**, to sign you in and to download the photos you
picked. Signing in and picking photos happen on Google's own pages, in your web browser. Glimpse
sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Glimpse
has no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Glimpse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will
adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your Mac until you delete it:

- **Remove a photo** in Glimpse, and its copy and thumbnail are deleted.
- **Turn off the screensaver option**, or choose **Turn off**, and the screensaver's copy of the
  photo is deleted.
- **Sign out**, and Glimpse deletes every copy of your photos (including the screensaver's), your
  thumbnails, your photo list, your email address, and your tokens. It also tells Google to
  cancel its access.
- **Uninstall Glimpse** and delete its folder, `~/Library/Application Support/Glimpse/`, to
  remove your settings and everything else it saved. If you used the screensaver, also delete
  `~/Library/Screen Savers/PhotoWallpaperSaver.saver`.

You can also cancel Glimpse's access to your Google account at any time at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Security

Your sign-in tokens are encrypted with a key held in your macOS Keychain. Your photos and other
data never leave your Mac, except to come from Google. No system is perfectly secure, so please
keep your Mac protected with a password.

## Children

Glimpse is not meant for children under 13. Google's own age rules apply to Google accounts.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, and California privacy law give
you rights to see, correct, and delete your personal information, and to object to its use.
Because we hold no personal information about you, all of your data is on your Mac and under
your control. You can see it in the folders above, and delete it as described in "Keeping and
deleting your data". If you have questions, contact us.

## Changes to this policy

announce important changes in the app's release notes.
