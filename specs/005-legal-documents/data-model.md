## Personal data inventory

The privacy policy's sections map one to one onto [research.md R1](./research.md). The rules are:
- every row in R1 appears in the policy, in plain words
- nothing appears in the policy that isn't in R1

**Sign-out deletion**, after the R2 fix: email, tokens, photo copies, thumbnails, selection
metadata, **and** the screensaver's photo copy. What stays until the app is uninstalled is the
settings and the original wallpaper paths. These are not personal data from Google.
